Next-Generation Bayesian Local Robustness

This talk comprises a trio of related current research projects in Bayesian
local robustness, with a focus on frequentist diagnostics of Bayesian
hierarchical regression modeling computed using Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC).
First, I present a novel Bayesian regression diagnostic based on checking for
invariance under covariate shift.  This diagnostic, and others like it, motivate
the development of fast, easy-to-use estimates of the frequentist variability of
MCMC procedures.  To this end, I use a von Mises expansion for Bayesian
posterior functionals to prove the asymptotic consistency of the infinitesimal
jackknife (IJ) variance estimator for smooth functions of posterior means.
Finally, in response to some theoretical and practical shortcomings of the IJ
estimator, I propose a flow-based approach to Bayesian local robustness drawing
on techniques from partial differential equations and optimal transport. In all
cases, I emphasize practical methods that are theoretically grounded but fast
and easy to compute from a single run of MCMC.
