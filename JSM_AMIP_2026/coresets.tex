\begin{frame}[t]{Do we identify ``the most important datapoints?''}

\def\S{\mathcal{S}}
Let $\S$ denote sets of data, $[N]$ denote all points, and $\delta(\S)$ the
change induced when dropping $\S$.\\[1em]

Informally, AMIP solves:
$$
\S_{AMIP} := \textrm{The smallest $\S$ with $\delta(\S) \ge \Delta$}
$$

Contrast this with the problem of finding a ``coreset'' \parencite{ting:2018:OptimalSubsamplingInfluence2018,chen:2024:CoresetMarkovChain2024}
$$
\begin{aligned}
\S_{NON-CORE} :={}& \textrm{The largest $\S$ with $\delta(\S) \le \Delta$},\textrm{ with }\\
\S_{CORE} :={}& [N] \setminus \S_{NON-CORE}.
\end{aligned}
$$

Both $\S_{AMIP}$ and $\S_{CORE}$ are ``important points,'' but in different senses. 

\end{frame}
